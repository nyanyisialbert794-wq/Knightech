Solar Panel Installation & Mounting

Support for rooftop and ground-mounted PV installation, with attention to alignment, secure mounting, cable routing, and tidy workmanship.

  • Review the installation area, roof type, access, shading, and safe working arrangements before work starts.
  • Support panel positioning and consistent spacing in line with the agreed layout.
  • Fit and fasten modules to the specified mounting system, checking that clamps and fixings are appropriate.
  • Coordinate rails, brackets, and mounting structures; identify any fabrication requirements before installation.
  • Keep cables routed neatly and protected from sharp edges, heat, and avoidable mechanical damage.

Solar System Planning & Components

Help understanding the main parts of a PV system and how generation, conversion, protection, and storage fit together.

  • Explain how PV modules convert sunlight into DC electricity and how an inverter supplies AC loads.
  • Identify the role of isolators, protection devices, charge controllers, batteries, and monitoring equipment.
  • Discuss daily energy use, peak demand, backup expectations, and how these affect system sizing.
  • Consider shading, orientation, roof space, ventilation, and safe access when planning a layout.
  • Prepare a component checklist and flag design questions for review by the responsible designer or installer.

Building Electrical Installation

Building-services electrical work support, with emphasis on orderly installation, circuit awareness, and following the required safety procedures.

  • Assist with conduit routes and cable containment planned for the building layout.
  • Support placement of lighting points, switches, socket outlets, and accessories according to drawings.
  • Understand the purpose of distribution boards, circuit labelling, protective devices, and earthing.
  • Keep cables, tools, and work areas organised to reduce damage and trip hazards.
  • Check completed work against the agreed scope and report any issues for qualified inspection and testing.

Maintenance & Troubleshooting

Structured first checks to help identify common performance issues and organise the next troubleshooting step.

  • Compare recent system output with normal performance and note the time, weather, and load conditions.
  • Look for visible shading, dust build-up, loose external covers, damaged conduit, or warning indicators.
  • Check monitoring readings and safely accessible user-level settings before drawing conclusions.
  • Record the symptom, when it began, and any recent changes so troubleshooting is more efficient.
  • Do not open live equipment or perform hazardous electrical tests; refer those checks to qualified personnel.

Work scope is confirmed per project. Electrical work, testing, and commissioning must follow applicable Kenyan standards and be performed by appropriately qualified and authorised personnel.

Responsive Website Development

Clean websites designed to work across phones, tablets, and desktop screens, with clear navigation and readable content.

  • Plan page sections around what a visitor needs to know first: service, value, evidence, and contact.
  • Build landing pages, portfolios, business profiles, and service pages with HTML and CSS.
  • Adapt layouts for phones, tablets, and desktop screens using responsive CSS.
  • Use readable typography, meaningful headings, clear calls to action, and keyboard-friendly navigation.
  • Optimise images and metadata so pages load efficiently and are easier to understand in search results.

Interactive Website Features

Useful interactions that help visitors explore information, contact a business, and move through a site naturally.

  • Create expandable service cards, accordions, tabs, and mobile navigation menus.
  • Build forms with required fields and clear confirmation or next-step messages.
  • Connect enquiry buttons to email, WhatsApp, or SMS links for straightforward contact workflows.
  • Add lightweight JavaScript for filters, interactive skill panels, sliders, and dynamic page details.
  • Test interactions with keyboard, touch, and common screen sizes.

Website Publishing & Maintenance

Support to prepare website files for publishing and keep content, links, and layout organised after launch.

  • Organise website files and publish static pages using an appropriate hosting platform.
  • Update text, images, contact details, links, and service information when business needs change.
  • Review page titles, descriptions, headings, image alt text, and link clarity.
  • Test pages in multiple browsers and screen widths after updates.
  • Keep a backup of the last working version before making larger changes.

Digital Design & Content

Visual structure and content presentation that help a brand communicate its services more clearly online.

  • Use spacing, contrast, headings, and card layouts to make important information easy to scan.
  • Keep colour, typography, icons, and buttons consistent with the brand identity.
  • Select suitable images, crop them thoughtfully, and use alt text for accessibility.
  • Write service descriptions that explain benefits, tasks, and the next action without overclaiming.
  • Check readability and visual balance on mobile as well as desktop.
