Solar Panel Installation & Mounting

Support for rooftop and ground-mounted PV installation, with attention to alignment, secure mounting, cable routing, and tidy workmanship.

  • Review the installation area, roof type, access, shading, and safe working arrangements before work starts.
  • Support panel positioning and consistent spacing in line with the agreed layout.
  • Fit and fasten modules to the specified mounting system, checking that clamps and fixings are appropriate.
  • Coordinate rails, brackets, and mounting structures; identify any fabrication requirements before installation.
  • Keep cables routed neatly and protected from sharp edges, heat, and avoidable mechanical damage.

Solar System Planning & Components

Help understanding the main parts of a PV system and how generation, conversion, protection, and storage fit together.

  • Explain how PV modules convert sunlight into DC electricity and how an inverter supplies AC loads.
  • Identify the role of isolators, protection devices, charge controllers, batteries, and monitoring equipment.
  • Discuss daily energy use, peak demand, backup expectations, and how these affect system sizing.
  • Consider shading, orientation, roof space, ventilation, and safe access when planning a layout.
  • Prepare a component checklist and flag design questions for review by the responsible designer or installer.

Building Electrical Installation

Building-services electrical work support, with emphasis on orderly installation, circuit awareness, and following the required safety procedures.

  • Assist with conduit routes and cable containment planned for the building layout.
  • Support placement of lighting points, switches, socket outlets, and accessories according to drawings.
  • Understand the purpose of distribution boards, circuit labelling, protective devices, and earthing.
  • Keep cables, tools, and work areas organised to reduce damage and trip hazards.
  • Check completed work against the agreed scope and report any issues for qualified inspection and testing.

Maintenance & Troubleshooting

Structured first checks to help identify common performance issues and organise the next troubleshooting step.

  • Compare recent system output with normal performance and note the time, weather, and load conditions.
  • Look for visible shading, dust build-up, loose external covers, damaged conduit, or warning indicators.
  • Check monitoring readings and safely accessible user-level settings before drawing conclusions.
  • Record the symptom, when it began, and any recent changes so troubleshooting is more efficient.
  • Do not open live equipment or perform hazardous electrical tests; refer those checks to qualified personnel.

Work scope is confirmed per project. Electrical work, testing, and commissioning must follow applicable Kenyan standards and be performed by appropriately qualified and authorised personnel.

Welding & Fabrication

Practical metalwork support for mounting frames, brackets, supports, and other project-specific structures, with careful measurement and finishing.

  • Measure and mark metal sections according to agreed dimensions and drawings.
  • Cut, prepare, align, and assemble metal components for frames, brackets, and supports.
  • Carry out welding and fabrication tasks appropriate to the material, joint, equipment, and agreed scope.
  • Check fit, alignment, stability, and finish before the fabricated item is put into service.
  • Use suitable PPE, fire precautions, ventilation, and a controlled work area; confirm structural requirements with the responsible professional where needed.

Solar Installation Steelwork & Mounting Fabrication

Fabrication and fitting support for solar PV mounting structures, panel-support frames, rails, brackets, base plates, and custom steel supports to the approved system design.

  • Measure and mark steel sections, rails, brackets, and plates against the approved layout and dimensions.
  • Cut, drill, prepare, weld, and assemble mounting frames, support legs, cross-members, braces, and connection plates where the design calls for fabricated steelwork.
  • Prepare base plates, anchor locations, clamp interfaces, and cable-clearance provisions so the mounting system suits the agreed PV layout.
  • Check squareness, alignment, spacing, fixing positions, and fit before components are transported or installed.
  • Prepare metal surfaces for the specified corrosion protection, and repair coating damage as directed.
  • Coordinate fabrication dimensions with panel sizes, roof type, wind-load requirements, access paths, drainage, and cable routing.
  • Use appropriate PPE, hot-work controls, safe lifting, and fall protection; keep sparks and metal debris away from modules and other equipment.

Mounting structures, anchors, welds, member sizes, and connections must follow the approved design and site requirements. Do not weld or modify roof members or an installed PV mounting system without authorisation. Electrical connections and PV commissioning are separate tasks.

Roofing Steelwork & Roof Installation

Fabrication and fitting support for roof trusses, purlins, roof frames, and iron-sheet roofing, with attention to alignment, fastening, and weather protection.

  • Measure the building footprint and check approved drawings, roof pitch, spans, levels, and material specifications before fabrication.
  • Measure, mark, cut, prepare, and assemble steel members to form roof trusses according to the approved design.
  • Position, align, space, and join purlins to the trusses; check straightness, level, spacing, and fixing points.
  • Fabricate and fit cleats, gusset plates, connection plates, brackets, base plates, stiffeners, and other specified supports.
  • Prepare roof members for lifting, assist with safe positioning and temporary bracing, and check alignment before final connections.
  • Install roof sheets in the correct sequence and overlap; fit appropriate roofing screws or fasteners with sealing washers as specified.
  • Fit ridge caps, barge flashings, valleys, eaves details, gutters, downpipe brackets, and flashing supports where included in the scope.
  • Check sheet alignment, fixings, cut edges, sharp projections, drainage paths, and potential leak points; apply specified corrosion protection and touch-up coatings.
  • Keep the roof area tidy and use suitable fall protection, safe access, lifting procedures, and fire precautions during hot work.

Structural member sizes, spans, connections, bracing, and lifting arrangements must follow an approved design and competent supervision. Do not cut or weld load-bearing roof members without authorisation.

Security & Architectural Metalwork

Custom-made steel doors, window grills, security gates, burglar bars, frames, and decorative metal features made to measured openings and the agreed design.

  • Take site measurements and confirm opening sizes, clearances, handing, swing direction, and fixing points before cutting material.
  • Fabricate steel door frames, security doors, window grills, burglar bars, gates, and protective screens.
  • Cut and join square/rectangular tubing, angle bars, flat bars, round bars, plates, and other specified sections.
  • Keep frames square and panels aligned; check diagonals, spacing, plumb, and level during assembly.
  • Fit hinges, hasps, locks, handles, latches, stops, rollers, guide channels, and mounting plates as required by the design.
  • Make decorative patterns, grill layouts, reinforcement pieces, and simple custom designs from the client's measurements or drawings.
  • Grind sharp edges and welds appropriately, check operation and clearances, and prepare surfaces for primer and paint or another specified finish.
  • Install and anchor finished items to suitable substrates using specified fixings; protect nearby glass, finishes, and property during installation.

Car Wash Bays & Steel Structures

Metal fabrication for car-wash shelters, bay dividers, equipment stands, hose supports, signage frames, and other practical service-area structures.

  • Fabricate steel columns, roof trusses, purlins, canopies, and shelter frames for car-wash bays to the approved layout.
  • Make and fit bay divider frames, protective rails, bollards, wheel stops, and splash-screen support frames where specified.
  • Build mounting brackets and stands for hose reels, pressure-washer accessories, signs, lighting, and small equipment.
  • Fabricate drain grates, removable covers, service platforms, and access steps when designed for the expected loads and wet environment.
  • Fit gutters and downpipe supports to direct roof runoff away from work areas and vehicle paths.
  • Use suitable corrosion-resistant materials or protective coatings for wet, chemical-exposed environments; seal or protect vulnerable joints as specified.
  • Check clear vehicle access, headroom, drainage routes, sharp edges, stability, and equipment clearances before handover.
  • Coordinate with plumbing and electrical trades so fabricated supports do not obstruct water lines, drainage, lighting, or electrical equipment.

Canopies, platforms, and load-bearing structures should be sized and approved by a competent professional. Electrical and pressure-washing equipment must be installed with appropriate protection for wet locations.

Gates, Railings, Stairs & Handrails

Fabrication of access and safety metalwork for homes, shops, workshops, and commercial premises.

  • Fabricate sliding and hinged gates, gate frames, guide brackets, stops, and support posts.
  • Make stair stringers, treads, landings, ladders, handrails, and guardrails to approved dimensions.
  • Produce balcony rails, rampside rails, protective barriers, and machine guards where required.
  • Check consistent spacing, smooth edges, alignment, clearances, and secure anchoring.
  • Prepare metal surfaces for galvanising, primer, paint, or other specified corrosion protection.

Stairs, guardrails, platforms, and other safety-critical items must follow applicable design and building requirements.

Repairs, Reinforcement & General Metalwork

Small fabrication jobs, modifications, repair work, and custom steel components for residential and commercial projects.

  • Repair non-critical metal frames, hinges, brackets, stands, trolleys, and equipment supports where safe and appropriate.
  • Fabricate shelves, workbenches, storage racks, tool stands, protective cages, and simple workshop fixtures.
  • Make sign frames, shade frames, clotheslines, refuse-bin stands, and custom mounting brackets.
  • Replace worn fasteners or damaged non-structural sections after checking the cause and repair suitability.
  • Measure, cut, drill, tack, weld, grind, deburr, clean, and finish components to the agreed specification.
  • Inspect weld appearance, dimensions, fit, and finish; refer cracks or damage to load-bearing components for competent assessment before repair.

The scope, materials, dimensions, finish, and safety requirements are agreed before work begins. Structural work should follow approved drawings and competent supervision; use appropriate PPE, hot-work precautions, ventilation, and fall protection.

Responsive Website Development

Clean websites designed to work across phones, tablets, and desktop screens, with clear navigation and readable content.

  • Plan page sections around what a visitor needs to know first: service, value, evidence, and contact.
  • Build landing pages, portfolios, business profiles, and service pages with HTML and CSS.
  • Adapt layouts for phones, tablets, and desktop screens using responsive CSS.
  • Use readable typography, meaningful headings, clear calls to action, and keyboard-friendly navigation.
  • Optimise images and metadata so pages load efficiently and are easier to understand in search results.

Interactive Website Features

Useful interactions that help visitors explore information, contact a business, and move through a site naturally.

  • Create expandable service cards, accordions, tabs, and mobile navigation menus.
  • Build forms with required fields and clear confirmation or next-step messages.
  • Connect enquiry buttons to email, WhatsApp, or SMS links for straightforward contact workflows.
  • Add lightweight JavaScript for filters, interactive skill panels, sliders, and dynamic page details.
  • Test interactions with keyboard, touch, and common screen sizes.

Website Publishing & Maintenance

Support to prepare website files for publishing and keep content, links, and layout organised after launch.

  • Organise website files and publish static pages using an appropriate hosting platform.
  • Update text, images, contact details, links, and service information when business needs change.
  • Review page titles, descriptions, headings, image alt text, and link clarity.
  • Test pages in multiple browsers and screen widths after updates.
  • Keep a backup of the last working version before making larger changes.

Digital Design & Content

Visual structure and content presentation that help a brand communicate its services more clearly online.

  • Use spacing, contrast, headings, and card layouts to make important information easy to scan.
  • Keep colour, typography, icons, and buttons consistent with the brand identity.
  • Select suitable images, crop them thoughtfully, and use alt text for accessibility.
  • Write service descriptions that explain benefits, tasks, and the next action without overclaiming.
  • Check readability and visual balance on mobile as well as desktop.
